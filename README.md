## 🚀 About FORTIFY

FORTIFY is an interactive mathematics platform envisioned to make mathematical exploration more intuitive, engaging, and accessible.

Traditional mathematical tools often focus on solving equations or plotting graphs. FORTIFY aims to go further by bringing together intelligent assistance, dynamic visualizations, real-world mathematical simulations, collaborative learning, and personalized practice in one unified environment.

Our mission is simple:

**Make mathematics something people experience, not just calculate.**

## ✨ Vision & Key Features

FORTIFY is designed around eight innovative capabilities:

### 1. MathPilot AI

An intelligent mathematical companion that helps users understand concepts, solve problems step by step, and explore alternative approaches.

### 2. Snap-to-Graph

Capture handwritten equations or mathematical expressions and transform them into interactive visualizations using image recognition.

### 3. Graph Time Machine

Explore how mathematical functions evolve by adjusting variables, parameters, and time-based changes.

### 4. Reality-to-Math Simulator

Connect mathematical concepts with real-world scenarios through interactive simulations and visual experiments.

### 5. LiveGraph

Enable multiple users to collaborate, edit graphs, and explore mathematical ideas together in real time.

### 6. Graph IQ

An adaptive learning experience that evaluates mathematical understanding and generates personalized challenges.

### 7. Math for Everyone

Accessibility-focused mathematical exploration with intuitive controls and inclusive learning experiences.

### 8. Math Art Studio

Transform mathematical equations and functions into creative, interactive mathematical artwork.

*These are the planned product capabilities; their availability depends on implementation progress.*

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

The final technology stack should reflect the actual implementation.

Potential technologies for the platform include:

- **Frontend:** React.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Mathematical Engine:** Math.js, Nerdamer
- **Graph Visualization:** JSXGraph / Custom Graph Renderer
- **3D Visualization:** Three.js
- **Backend:** Node.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL / Supabase
- **Real-time Collaboration:** WebSockets
- **AI Integration:** LLM APIs
- **Deployment:** Vercel
